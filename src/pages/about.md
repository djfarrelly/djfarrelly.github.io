Hey, I'm Dan. I co-founded [Inngest](https://www.inngest.com) in 2021 and spent the last several years as CTO leading product, technical and growth functions at the company. I'm passionate about developer tools, software architecture, and more recently agentic system architecture. Lately, I'm most interested in the space of deploying reliable, observable, and secure AI products. I also do select fractional work or advising; [send me a message](https://www.linkedin.com/in/djfarrelly/) or [book an intro](https://cal.com/dan-farrelly/intro) if you'd like to chat.

Earlier in my career, I was CTO at [Buffer](https://buffer.com) and I created the open source developer tool [MailDev](https://maildev.github.io/maildev/) (⭐︎6k+) that I still maintain over a decade later.

I also speak at conferences and chat with folks on podcasts or in the press now and then — the highlights are on the [speaking & press page](/speaking-and-press/).

Outside of work, I love [working to restore](https://www.instagram.com/michigancraftsman/) my 100+ year old craftsman bungalow, from restoring old windows, doing electrical work, to refinishing wood doors and trim. I mentor my nephew's robotics team and also love biking, hiking, and camping.
